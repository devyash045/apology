# For Someone Precious — Digital Apology Experience 💌

A heartfelt, interactive, and emotional digital apology website created with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features ✨
- **Ambient Visuals:** Soft romantic color palette, gentle floating particles, glowing orbs, and glassmorphic UI.
- **Synthesized Audio Chimes:** Built-in Web Audio API gentle chimes and romantic harmonic chords without external audio files.
- **Emotional Storytelling:** 
  - Section 1: The Opening (A Message for You)
  - Section 2: Accountability (No Excuses)
  - Section 3: Reflection (Your Feelings, Your Trust, Your Smile)
  - Section 4: Memories Gallery (Custom Vector Polaroid Frames)
  - Section 5: A Moment of Honesty (Interactive Test)
  - Section 6: Commitments & Promises
  - Section 7: The Emotional Climax
  - Section — Love Letter: Teaser surprise card with smooth unfolding letter to *“To My Wiffee Ji ❤️”* signed by *“Yash”*
  - Section 8: Final Apology
  - Section 9: Reminders of Love (Interactive Reason Generator)
  - Section 10: Final Screen
- **100% Mobile Responsive:** Optimized across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
