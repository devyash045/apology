Likha hai dil ki har ek dhadkan se, sirf aapke liye ✦

Main aapse bohot beinteha pyaar karta hoon — itna ki shabdon me bayaan karna bhi kam pad jaye. Mujhe achhe se maalum hai ki mujhse galti hui hai, aur meri laparwahi ki wajah se aapka dil dukhha hai.
